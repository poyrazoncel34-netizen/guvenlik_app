## 🇬🇧 ENGLISH

### App Name
KoruBeni — Personal Safety

### Short Description (80 characters)
Call for help with one touch. Share your location. Stay safe, always.

### Full Description (4000 character limit)

KoruBeni is a comprehensive personal safety app designed to keep you protected. In an emergency, call for help with a single touch, share your location with trusted contacts, and feel safe wherever you are.

🛡️ EMERGENCY BUTTON
Hold the panic button and release. If you don't enter your PIN within 10 seconds, an emergency SMS with your location is automatically sent and a call is made to your emergency contact.

📍 LIVE LOCATION SHARING
Share your location with trusted contacts while walking, jogging, or traveling alone. Location updates are sent at regular intervals.

🚶 SAFE WALK
Enter your expected arrival time. If you don't check in when time is up, an emergency is automatically triggered.

📞 FAKE CALL
Receive a fake incoming call when you feel unsafe. Customize the caller name and photo. The perfect excuse to leave an uncomfortable situation.

🔊 SIREN
Activate a loud siren to attract attention in dangerous situations.

🎤 AUDIO RECORDING
Record your surroundings as evidence. Audio files are stored only on your device and never uploaded to servers.

📳 SHAKE DETECTION
Trigger an emergency by rapidly shaking your phone. Cancel by entering your PIN.

🔐 SECURITY & PRIVACY
• PIN code + biometric authentication (fingerprint / Face ID)
• AES encryption for data protection
• KVKK and GDPR compliant

🌍 MULTILINGUAL
Turkish and English language support.

📡 OFFLINE SUPPORT
If you're offline, emergency events are queued and automatically synced when your connection is restored.

Your safety in your hands. Feel protected at all times with KoruBeni.
