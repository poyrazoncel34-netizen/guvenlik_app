**Subtitle:** Personal Safety & Emergency Support

**Promotional Text:**
Your safety is one tap away. With KoruBeni, reach your loved ones instantly in emergencies.

**Description:**
KoruBeni is a personal safety app that puts your security first, helping you quickly connect with your safety network in emergencies.

With the panic button, emergency contact calling, quick messaging, and location sharing, you get support with a single tap. You can even shake your phone to trigger an emergency.

CORE FEATURES:

• Panic Button — Hold or shake to trigger emergency
• Emergency Contact Calling — Automatic calls and notifications to selected contacts
• Location Sharing — Live location for 10min, 30min, or 1 hour
• Safe Walk — Location tracking for night or unsafe situations
• Fake Call — Realistic incoming call to escape uncomfortable situations
• Voice Recording — Evidence recording in emergencies
• Siren — Loud alarm to draw attention
• Quick Message — One-tap message templates

SECURITY:

• 4-digit PIN app protection
• Face ID / Touch ID support
• AES-256 encryption for data security
• Firebase security rules protect all data

KoruBeni keeps your safety with you at all times.
