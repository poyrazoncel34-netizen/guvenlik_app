KoruBeni Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 19, 2026 — Version 3.0.0

KoruBeni ("App", "we", "our") is a personal safety application designed to protect its users. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

KoruBeni works offline-first. The primary copy of your data stays on your device and is not sent to a developer-operated cloud backend. No data is transferred to third-party servers.

1. Age Restriction

KoruBeni is intended for users aged 18 and older. The app performs age verification during onboarding. Users under 18 may only use the app with explicit parental or legal guardian consent, in accordance with KVKK Article 6 (processing of personal data of minors requires parental consent). If we become aware that a user under 18 is using the app without parental consent, we will take steps to delete their data from the device.

2. Data We Collect

a) User-Provided Data

  • Emergency contacts: Names and phone numbers of contacts selected from your device
  • Profile information: Name and profile photo (optional)
  • PIN code: Stored encrypted on-device only, never sent to any server
  • Audio recordings: Ambient audio recordings created by the user during emergency situations, stored on-device only
  • Fake call data: Caller name and configuration for the fake call feature, stored on-device only

b) Automatically Collected Data

  • Location data: Only when you actively start a location session or trigger an emergency
  • Device-side runtime state: Temporary technical state needed for app flows
  • Legal consent log: Timestamps and versions of your consent to KVKK and privacy policies, stored on-device only

c) Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Anonymous usage statistics
  • Server-side crash reports
  • Message contents
  • Call logs
  • Browsing history
  • Financial information
No Developer Backend: No data is sent to developer-operated servers or cloud databases. Map, Play Billing, and explicitly configured crash reporting SDK traffic is governed by those providers.

3. Audio Recording

KoruBeni includes an audio recording feature for emergency situations. Audio is recorded locally on the device and is never uploaded to any server.

Legal Warning (TCK Article 133): Recording private conversations of others without their consent is a criminal offense under Turkish Penal Code Article 133 and may result in 2 to 5 years of imprisonment. KoruBeni's audio recording feature is intended solely for the user's personal safety in emergency situations. The user bears full legal responsibility for how they use this feature. Do not record conversations without the knowledge and consent of all parties involved.

4. How We Use Data

  • Start the Android call flow for your designated emergency contact
  • Support Safe Walk and check-in flows
  • Show local notifications
  • Protect app access with PIN authentication
  • Record audio during emergencies (user-initiated only)
  • Simulate fake incoming calls for discreet exit from dangerous situations
Important: Your location data is only processed when you start a location session or when an emergency is triggered. There is no continuous background location tracking.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data to third parties. No data is sent to a developer-operated backend. Third-party SDK network behavior is limited to map, Play Billing, and explicitly configured crash reporting services.

  • Emergency contacts: When an emergency is triggered, the Android call flow is started or the dialer is prepared for your selected contact
  • Map providers: OpenStreetMap infrastructure may be contacted when map screens are opened
  • Legal requirements: When required by law or court order

6. Emergency Contacts — Third-Party Data Subject Rights

When you add emergency contacts, you store their names and phone numbers on your device. These individuals are also data subjects under KVKK Article 11 and have the following rights:

  • Right to be informed: Emergency contacts have the right to know that their data is stored in this app
  • Right to request deletion: Emergency contacts may request the removal of their data
  • Right to object: Emergency contacts may object to the processing of their data

It is the user's responsibility to inform their emergency contacts that their name and phone number are stored in this app and to obtain their consent where applicable. To exercise these rights, the data subject may contact us at the email address listed below.

7. Data Security

  • Sensitive data is protected with AES-256 encryption
  • PIN codes are stored in secure device storage (Keychain/Keystore)
  • The primary copy of sensitive data remains on the device
  • No data is sent to external servers or cloud backends

8. Data Retention

  • Profile data: Until the user deletes it on the device
  • Emergency contacts: Until the user deletes them on the device
  • Audio recordings: Until the user deletes them on the device
  • Location data: Used only during active emergency and sharing flows
  • Legal consent log: Retained on-device for the duration of app usage

10. Your Rights (KVKK Article 11)

  • Access: Request access to your data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Delete app data by clearing storage or uninstalling the app
  • Permission management: Change location, contacts, microphone, and notification permissions at any time
  • Data portability: Request a copy of your data
  • Right to object: Object to processing of your data
  • Right to complain: File a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK Kurulu)
