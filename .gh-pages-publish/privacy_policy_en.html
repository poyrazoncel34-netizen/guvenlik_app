KoruBeni Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 26, 2026

KoruBeni ("App", "we", "our") is a personal safety application designed to protect its users. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Data We Collect

a) User-Provided Data

  • Phone number: For account creation and authentication
  • Emergency contacts: Names and phone numbers of contacts selected from your device
  • Profile information: Name and profile photo (optional)
  • PIN code: Stored encrypted on-device only, never sent to our servers

b) Automatically Collected Data

  • Location data: Only when you actively enable location sharing or trigger an emergency
  • Device information: OS version, app version (for crash reporting)
  • Usage statistics: Anonymous app usage data (Firebase Analytics)
  • Crash reports: App crash data (Firebase Crashlytics)

c) Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Message contents
  • Call logs
  • Browsing history
  • Financial information

2. How We Use Data

  • Send location and alerts to designated emergency contacts
  • Track location during Safe Walk sessions
  • Send push notifications
  • Improve app reliability and performance
  • Detect and fix bugs
Important: Your location data is only collected when you actively share it or when an emergency is triggered. There is no continuous background location tracking.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data to third parties. Data is shared only in these cases:

  • Emergency contacts: When an emergency is triggered, your location and status are sent to your selected contacts
  • Service providers: Firebase (Google) infrastructure services (under data processing agreements)
  • Legal requirements: When required by law or court order

4. Data Security

  • Sensitive data is protected with AES-256 encryption
  • PIN codes are stored in secure device storage (Keychain/Keystore)
  • All communication is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
  • Firebase security rules prevent unauthorized access
  • Biometric authentication (Face ID/Touch ID) support

5. Data Retention

  • Account data: Until account deletion
  • Location data: Automatically deleted when sharing period expires
  • Emergency records: Automatically deleted after 90 days
  • Crash reports: Automatically deleted after 180 days

6. Your Rights

  • Access: Request access to your data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all associated data
  • Permission management: Change location, contacts, and notification permissions at any time
  • Data portability: Request a copy of your data

7. Children's Privacy

KoruBeni does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided data, we will immediately delete it.

8. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, database, notifications, analytics, crash reporting
  • OpenStreetMap: Map display (no personal data collected)

Their respective privacy policies apply:

  • Firebase Privacy Policy
  • OpenStreetMap Privacy Policy

9. Policy Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When significant changes are made, users will be notified through in-app notifications.
