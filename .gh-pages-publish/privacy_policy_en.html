KoruBeni Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 7, 2026

KoruBeni ("App", "we", "our") is a personal safety application designed to protect its users. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

KoruBeni works offline-first. The primary copy of your data stays on your device and is not sent to a developer-operated cloud backend.

1. Data We Collect

a) User-Provided Data

  • Emergency contacts: Names and phone numbers of contacts selected from your device
  • Profile information: Name and profile photo (optional)
  • PIN code: Stored encrypted on-device only, never sent to any server

b) Automatically Collected Data

  • Location data: Only when you actively enable location sharing or trigger an emergency
  • Device-side runtime state: Temporary technical state needed for app flows

c) Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Anonymous usage statistics
  • Server-side crash reports
  • Message contents
  • Call logs
  • Browsing history
  • Financial information

2. How We Use Data

  • Send location and alerts to designated emergency contacts via SMS
  • Support Safe Walk and check-in flows
  • Show local notifications
  • Protect app access with PIN and biometric authentication
Important: Your location data is only collected when you actively share it or when an emergency is triggered. There is no continuous background location tracking.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data to third parties. Data is shared only in these cases:

  • Emergency contacts: When an emergency is triggered, your location and status are sent to your selected contacts via SMS
  • Map providers: OpenStreetMap infrastructure may be contacted when map screens are opened
  • Legal requirements: When required by law or court order

4. Data Security

  • Sensitive data is protected with AES-256 encryption
  • PIN codes are stored in secure device storage (Keychain/Keystore)
  • The primary copy of sensitive data remains on the device
  • Biometric authentication (Face ID/Touch ID) support

5. Data Retention

  • Profile data: Until the user deletes it on the device
  • Emergency contacts: Until the user deletes them on the device
  • Audio recordings: Until the user deletes them on the device
  • Location data: Used only during active emergency and sharing flows

6. Your Rights

  • Access: Request access to your data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Delete app data by clearing storage or uninstalling the app
  • Permission management: Change location, contacts, and notification permissions at any time
  • Data portability: Request a copy of your data

7. Children's Privacy

KoruBeni does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Third-Party Services

  • OpenStreetMap: Map display

Their respective privacy policies apply:

  • OpenStreetMap Privacy Policy

9. Policy Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When significant changes are made, users will be notified through in-app notifications.
